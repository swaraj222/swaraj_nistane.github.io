  <div><p class="eyebrow">About</p><h1>I study how teams of robots can make good decisions with imperfect information.</h1></div>
  <p>I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Georgia, advised by <a href="https://www.cs.uga.edu/directory/people/ramviyas-nattanmai-parasuraman" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Dr. Ramviyas Parasuraman</a> in the Heterogeneous Robotics (HeRo) Lab.</p>

  <div class="prose"><h2>My path</h2><p>My work focuses on distributed planning and control for multi-UAV systems, particularly coverage, tracking, and informative path planning. I am interested in methods that are theoretically grounded, computationally practical, and ready to be tested beyond simulation.</p><p>I earned a BS–MS in Physics from the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research Bhopal. My master’s work explored autonomous underwater-vehicle guidance for target tracking, building a bridge from dynamics and control to my current research in multi-robot autonomy.</p><p>Today, I investigate how aerial robot teams can balance competing needs: exploring large areas, taking high-quality measurements, coordinating with limited communication, and operating safely around one another.</p></div>
</section>
